Frequently Asked Questions

Common Refinancing Questions

Benefits of Refinancing

What are the benefits of refinancing my auto loan?

Refinancing can lower your interest rate, reduce your monthly payment, or shorten your loan term — potentially saving you money over the life of the loan.

Will refinancing hurt my credit score?

Checking your rate uses a soft credit pull and does not affect your score. A hard inquiry only occurs if you proceed with a full application.

Rates & Terms

How is my rate determined?

Your rate depends on your credit profile, loan term, and the age and mileage of your vehicle.

What loan terms are available?

Terms range from 12 to 84 months depending on your loan amount and vehicle eligibility.

Eligibility & Application

What vehicles are eligible for refinancing?

Most personal-use cars, trucks, and SUVs up to 10 model years old with under 120,000 miles are eligible.

What documents do I need?

You'll need your current loan statement, proof of insurance, and your vehicle's VIN and mileage.
